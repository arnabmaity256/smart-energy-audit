PZEM Mains Hookup

How to connect the PZEM-004T v3.0 to mains through an extension board without cutting any conductor. The load current keeps flowing through the board as usual. The CT clamp only sits around the live core, and two fused tap wires give the PZEM its voltage.

230 V can kill. Work only with a lab instructor present.
  • Everything stays unplugged while you wire, measure or change anything.
  • Plug in only after the instructor has inspected the closed enclosure.
  • Use a socket protected by an RCCB/ELCB, and never touch the PZEM board while it is plugged in.

The complete setup

Everything inside the hatched outline is at mains potential and lives inside the closed plastic enclosure. Only the four low-voltage TTL wires leave it.

Where the CT clamp goes

A current transformer measures the magnetic field of the current passing through it. Live and neutral carry the same current in opposite directions, so it must go around one core only.

Identify the cores

India uses two colour schemes. Confirm which core is live with your instructor or a continuity test from the plug pins. Cheap boards are sometimes miswired.

First power-on

Hands off the enclosure. Watch the Uno's Serial Monitor at 9600 baud, or ask Claude to watch COM10.

1 · No load

Plug in with nothing in the sockets. The error frames turn into data frames with voltage and zero current.

$E,PZEM_NO_RESPONSE*69      before
$D,1,229.8,0.000,0.0,0.000,50.0,0.00*..   after

2 · With the 100 W bulb

Switch the board off, plug in the bulb, switch on. Expect roughly:

V  ≈ 230 V     I  ≈ 0.43 A
P  ≈ 95–100 W  PF ≈ 1.00
f  ≈ 50.0 Hz

If current stays at 0 with the bulb on, the CT is around more than one core or not fully closed. Unplug before you touch it.
